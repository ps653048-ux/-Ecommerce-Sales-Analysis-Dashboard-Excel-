📊 -Ecommerce-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard-Excel
The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, profit, orders, quantity, and profitability across categories, regions, and time.
The goal of this project is to demonstrate how Excel can be effectively used for end-to-end data analysis and reporting, from raw data to actionable insights.

🛠 Tools & Techniques Used
1) Microsoft Excel. 
2) Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts. 
3) KPI Cards (Sales, Profit, Orders, Quantity, Profit Margin). 
4) Year-over-Year (YoY) Analysis. 
5) Slicers & Interactive Filters. 
6) Data Cleaning & Formatting. 
7) Dashboard Design & Layout. 
   
📈 Key Business Insights

1) Total sales reached $2.29M with a 20.62% YoY growth, indicating strong revenue performance.
2) Despite higher order volume (+28.64% YoY), profit margin declined by 5.15%, highlighting cost or discount pressure.
3) Technology emerged as the most profitable category (~$145K profit), while Furniture showed very low profitability despite sales contribution.
4) Phones and Chairs are the top-performing sub-categories by sales value.
5) Sales show a clear year-end peak, suggesting seasonal buying behavior.
Regional analysis reveals West and East regions as major revenue contributors.
🧩 Dashboard Features
1) Dynamic KPI tracking with YoY comparison. 
2) Category-wise sales and profit analysis. 
3) State-wise sales visualization using map chart. 
4) Top 10 sub-category performance ranking. 
5) Interactive filtering by Region, Segment, and Year. 

🎯 Learnings & Outcomes
1) Improved understanding of business-oriented data analysis. 
2) Hands-on experience with interactive Excel dashboards. 
3) Learned how to identify profitability issues beyond sales growth. 
4) Strengthened data storytelling and insight extraction skills.
